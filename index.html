My On-the-Job Training journey at Inksite Advertising.

🖨️ On-the-Job Training

Inksite Advertising · 2023

In 2023, I completed my On-the-Job Training at Inksite Advertising, a full-service printing and advertising company. Over four months, I worked directly with the company's manager and senior developers, transitioning from classroom projects to building 9 distinct production-grade tools that the business still relies on today.

I was fully embedded in their workflow—attending daily stand-ups, gathering requirements from the operations team, and shipping features on a two-week sprint cycle. I built pricing calculators (tarpaulin, Sintra board, custom t-shirts), a Point-of-Sale system, a dental clinic management system, a photography booking portal, an inventory module, a content creation CMS, and several promotional web games—each one replacing messy spreadsheets and whiteboard tracking.

By the end of my internship, my supervisor noted my ability to deliver ahead of schedule, adapt quickly to their existing tech stack, and communicate clearly with non-technical staff—making me a reliable contributor from day one. Several of these tools are still in active use at the company today.
